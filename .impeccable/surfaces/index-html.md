## Scope

`index.html`, the whole single-page app. Visitor mode: Operate. States: empty (choose a recording), checking, results with live before/after, saving with progress, done (download or share to Photos), errors (unsupported file, device out of memory, missing codec), and record-better tips. Simple by default; an Adjust panel holds pro controls.

## Audience and job

ASMR artists, phone and desktop equally, clips under 10 minutes and sessions up to 60+ minutes (multi-GB). Job: learn in plain words what the phone did to the sound, hear the fix, save the same video with corrected audio.

## Memorable moment

Swap: the pads are the ears and never move. Under them, the "recorded left" and "recorded right" tags slide past each other and trade places while the preview keeps playing, so the corrected state still reads L over the left ear. (Changed after the first finish review: pads trading places made the fixed state read as broken.)

## Open decisions

Final fix list and video-side fixes wait on `reports/`. Output codec per browser (AAC or PCM in MOV) waits on hands-on testing. Localisation not decided.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The L and R every listener checks before pressing play become the interface. Refuses the dark DAW with a neon waveform and the pastel moon-and-clouds "sleepy ASMR" page.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral graphite soft-touch ground; two molded earcup pads, cobalt L and vermilion R, letters debossed at monumental scale, rendered with real lighting from a height map; a raised tactile dot on the left pad; purple only where both channels carry the same sound (waveform and meters). One family (Barlow) sets letters and captions on one grid. Fix states and recommended settings are stamped.

STORY: The artist drops a take, reads what the phone did, hears before and after, taps Fix, saves the same video with corrected sound, and leaves with two settings for next time.

FIRST VIEWPORT: L pad left, R pad right, about 56 to 60% of viewport height, letters filling them. A round socket on the seam holds the primary "Choose a recording", the drop hint (pointer devices), and "Never uploaded". One plain sentence below says what it fixes.

FORM: Molded L / R, #3 of my 7; seed key dd3e437c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
